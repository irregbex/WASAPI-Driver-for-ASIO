2. Hearing and equipment safety
-------------------------------
- An audio driver can produce very loud sound: a wrong gain setting, a
  clipped signal in Exclusive mode, a dropout or a host bug can send
  full-scale noise or clicks to your speakers or headphones. Start with a
  low monitoring volume, especially with headphones and in-ear monitors.
- The latency test (WasapiDriverTest.exe --latency) deliberately plays a
  loud click every second. Turn the volume down before running it.
- Loopback inputs can create acoustic or digital feedback: with Loopback
  "All audio of the output device", monitoring the loopback track feeds the
  output back into itself and can build up to full scale. Use "Everything
  except this DAW" when you need to monitor, and never monitor a microphone
  through speakers in the same room at high gain.
- The "Gain (dB)" setting can raise the output by up to +24 dB.
Prolonged exposure to high sound levels can permanently damage hearing.
High-level signals can damage speakers.
